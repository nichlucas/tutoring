About me

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the University of British Columbia, working within the Advanced Materials and Process Engineering Laboratory (AMPEL) to help push the limits of advanced alloy development. My work is computational in nature and aims to better understand the physics accompanying a complex phase transformation observed in some steels. I previously carried out research during my Ph.D. at the University of Cambridge (Earth Sciences), where I worked with metamorphic rocks to study the evolution of mountain ranges. My career has so-far enabled me to study the thermochemical evolution both natural and manufactured materials at high-temperature conditions!

My tutoring experience primarily comes from my time as a PhD researcher at the University of Cambridge (2021-2025), where I worked with over 100 undergraduate students in tutorials, laboratory sessions and during field studies. I provided small-group "supervisions"—a hallmark of the Oxbridge system—designed for personalized guidance, to foster deep subject understanding, and develop critical thinking beyond the core lecture material. I have helped students falling behind and those already excelling, and I've learned a lot in the process.

I provide premium tutoring up to the undergraduate level as well as university admissions coaching and interview preparation. Whether you're falling behind, reaching for top grades or just needing some guidance, I can help you. I'm currently accepting in-person clients in Vancouver (Kitsilano/UBC) and virtually.
